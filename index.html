Do I need any experience or an audience?

No. Every course starts from the basics and assumes you're beginning from zero. You don't need followers, a website, or design skills to begin.

How do I get access after paying?

After you send payment via GCash, you'll share your payment screenshot and email. Once it's confirmed, you'll receive your course access details. See the payment page for the exact steps.

Is this a one-time payment?

Yes. Each course is a single one-time payment with lifetime access — no subscriptions or hidden fees.

Can I get a refund?

No. All sales are final. Because these are digital products delivered instantly, we don't offer refunds once your payment is confirmed. Please make sure you've picked the right course before you pay — message us first if you're unsure.

Can I take the courses on my phone?

Yes. The lessons work on any device with a browser, so you can learn on your phone, tablet, or laptop.

Which course should I start with?

If you're brand new, the RaketPH mini course is the easiest entry point. If you want the full picture from idea to first sale, go with the Digital Product Creation Course.
